# Design Tokens Foundation — Plan Brief

> Full plan: `context/changes/design-tokens-foundation/plan.md`

## What & Why

This change gives Menu Maker one visual contract. `resources/css/app.css` moves to the warm OKLCH palette in `context/foundation/design-system.md`:

- herb green as the only brand colour;
- state tokens and meal-category colours;
- a new radius;
- a `.dark` block.

It also adds the six missing shadcn-vue primitives, gives `badge` and `alert` category and state variants, and writes the design-system rule into `CLAUDE.md`. This is F-03 in roadmap M-2. Every later view migration needs these tokens before it can drop literal colours.

## Starting Point

- `app.css` has grey HSL shadcn tokens (`--primary` is almost black), a dead `gray-200` border layer and unused chart tokens.
- `ui/` holds 15 legacy "new-york" components. `badge` has no category or state variants, and there is no `alert`, `alert-dialog`, `checkbox`, `skeleton`, `collapsible` or `pagination`.

## Desired End State

- The whole app picks up a cream background, green primary buttons and links, a green focus ring and warm borders the moment the tokens land. Views that still use literals keep their colours until their own slice.
- New primitives and variants (`<Badge variant="breakfast">`, `<Alert variant="warning">`) are ready for S-07…S-14.
- `CLAUDE.md` tells agents to use tokens, not palette classes.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- `app.css` tokens, both light and dark: base, 3 state pairs, 3 meal pairs, radius.
- The six primitives (`alert-dialog`, `alert`, `checkbox`, `skeleton`, `collapsible`, `pagination`).
- `badge` variants (`breakfast`, `supper`, `dinner`, `success`, `warning`, `info`, plus a `rounded-full` base) and `alert` variants (`warning`, `info`, `success`).
- The `CLAUDE.md` rule.
- Updates to design-system §4.8/§5.1/§8/§9 and the F-04 roadmap note.

**Out of scope:**

- Migrating views or domain components (S-07…S-13).
- The kitchen sink, screenshot gate and `lint:tokens` (F-04).
- The PDF (S-14).
- A dark-mode toggle.
- Regenerating the legacy `ui/` components, or `@lucide/vue`.

## Architecture / Approach

`:root` and `.dark` hold full `oklch()` values, and `@theme inline` maps each one to `--color-*`, which gives utilities like `bg-meal-dinner` and `text-warning-foreground`. Token names stay shadcn-standard, so the existing `ui/` components repaint with no edits. New primitives are generated by the CLI, then their imports are pointed back at the repo's own `cn` and `lucide-vue-next`.

## Phases at a Glance

| Phase | What it delivers | Key risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. OKLCH tokens in app.css | New palette live app-wide, with state and domain tokens available | Visual regressions; checked by E2E and a manual pass over key views |
| 2. Primitives and variants | 6 v4 primitives without new packages; `badge`/`alert` variants | The CLI's `reka-ui` bump affecting existing dialogs and selects; checked by E2E and a `package.json` review |
| 3. Docs and rule | `CLAUDE.md` rule; design-system and roadmap in sync | None significant |

**Prerequisites:** the M-2 roadmap rewrite and this change folder committed as `docs:` before Phase 1; Sail running (`vendor/bin/sail up -d`, including the queue and Mailpit), `.env` with `AI_SCALING_DRIVER=fake`, and network access inside the container for `npx shadcn-vue@2`.

**Estimated effort:** about 1 session across 3 small phases.

## Open Risks & Assumptions

- Assumption: the CLI leaves `app.css` and `components.json` alone for these six items (none carries `cssVars`). Phase 2 verifies this and reverts any hunk.
- The new v4 primitives aren't rendered anywhere until F-04's kitchen sink, so for now they are checked only by the build and a code read.
- Until the D-9 follow-up, `ui/` mixes two styles (6 v4, 15 legacy).

## Success Criteria (Summary)

- The app shows the warm, green-branded palette everywhere, with no lost borders and with E2E green.
- `<Badge variant="breakfast|supper|dinner|success|warning|info">` and `<Alert variant="warning|info|success">` work using tokens only, and no new npm packages landed.
- Agents reading `CLAUDE.md` are told to use tokens and existing primitives instead of literals.
